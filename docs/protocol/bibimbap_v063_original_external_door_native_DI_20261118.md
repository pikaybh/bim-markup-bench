## Independent physically grounded native IFC gate (structural, NOT public-answer grader)

The source-only native admission checks, with *no original author evaluation or public question answer target* in its logic:

1. Exact original IFC SHA and schema/units; output must be distinct regular IFC4X3; **all 416 original STEP IDs and native attributes/Root GUIDs/geometry data must remain intact**. Output has exactly the original 7 Walls/2 Slabs/9 Wall joins, exactly one new Door, one new Opening, one `IfcRelFillsElement`, one `IfcRelVoidsElement`, and one Door storey containment.
2. One valid new Door→Opening→original Wall relationship chain, real new 3D Door/Opening meshes with door panel fully inside opening; door `OverallWidth` and `OverallHeight` expressed in **native mm** must correspond to the actual world-metre bounding sizes.
3. The new Opening must lie inside its original host Wall span away from existing joins, sit on the original finished-floor level, be below the original Wall top, and **penetrate past both full-thickness Wall faces**. Original source Wall shape volume minus actual output Wall shape volume must be equal, to strict numerical tolerance, to the expected opening-width × source-wall-thickness × opening-height intersection. **A fills/voids relationship alone with shallow or non-cutting geometry is not sufficient.**
4. All other ORIGINAL product world mesh bounding boxes and measured solid volumes are unchanged; candidate adds zero new source-vs-output IFC4X3 schema-validator findings. Source native STEP modifications are not silently allowed.
5. Exactly five new rooted elements are permitted: `IfcDoor`, `IfcOpeningElement`, `IfcRelVoidsElement`, `IfcRelFillsElement`, and `IfcRelContainedInSpatialStructure`. Every other added non-Root STEP entity must be recursively reachable from the permitted new Roots. This **graph-closure** check rejects unrelated added Root objects and orphaned anonymous native geometry.

The native gate deliberately allows **physically structured interior-host Doors** and undersized Doors when their full CSG geometry/host chain is consistent. It does **not** know whether a wall is on the outer envelope nor enforce the original question's reasonable Door dimensions. That public physical criterion is a separate evaluator-only `source_public_exterior_pass_v063` result. It identifies original boundary walls by the physical outer envelope, checks true full-thickness cut, and applies explicit plausible door dimensions; **without IfcSpace the actual indoor/outdoor circulation semantics remain unverified**.

The oracle is conservative in preserving all original STEP IDs, native attributes, and strict new-entity graph; alternative compliant IFC authoring styles (e.g. regenerating original Wall CSG Body representations with different STEP ids, different spatial containment conventions, different new relation structures) may be rejected. This potentially **biases against unrestricted general I-native Python** and must not be portrayed as universal IFC equivalence or as a fair comparable LLM trial. Internal-wall Door created in a valid CSG is **structurally valid** and distinguished from **public physical exterior correctness**.
